Scope of this example

Changes are limited to a 60 Hz high-pass filter, two-pass loudness normalization, resampling to 48 kHz and 24-bit PCM export. No words were reordered or removed; no pause, breath or timing edits were applied.

This study does not demonstrate noise restoration, detailed dialogue editing, transcription accuracy or long-form consistency. Measurement and browser playback checks do not establish listening quality or buyer acceptance.
